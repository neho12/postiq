Concept exploration

Three directions, one decision.

Each direction was drawn as real geometry, tested at 16 px, in one colour and on dark, and scored on four criteria from 1 to 5. Generic solutions were rejected, not polished.

DECISION

Why the open ring.

Concept B alone gives the identity an ownable mark with a story: the O opens and an idea steps out. Concept C is not discarded: its capitals are the wordmark of the final system. Concept A is rejected: it puts a letter in a circle, which has been done.
