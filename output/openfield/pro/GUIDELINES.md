# Openfield: logo guidelines

**Idea:** a circle (the "o", the field) with one segment lifted out: a bead that steps out into open space.
The bead is the brand's only accent. It repeats as the dot of the "i" in the wordmark.

## Files
- `logo-horizontal-*` primary lockup. `logo-stacked-*` for square spaces.
- `symbol-*` the mark alone (avatars, social). `icon-*` app icons. `favicon.svg` for browser tabs.
- Variants: `color-light` (for light backgrounds), `color-dark` (for dark backgrounds), `black`, `white`, `purple`.

## Colour
- Ink #0B0B0D, white #FFFFFF, accent purple #8B5CF6 (on light) / lime #C4F25E (on dark only).
- The accent is used only for the bead and the i-dot, never for large areas.

## Clear space and size
- Keep clear space equal to the bead's diameter on all sides.
- Minimum size: symbol 16 px, horizontal lockup 80 px wide. Below that use the symbol only.

## Do not
- Recolour the ring, change the gap, add effects, rotate the bead, or set the wordmark in another font.

## Type
- Wordmark set in Sora SemiBold (SIL Open Font License), converted to outlines. Verify trademark clearance before registering.
